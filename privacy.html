1. What We Collect

Account data: your name, email address and optional phone number. Financial data you enter: income, expenses, categories, budgets, savings goals, grocery items and any receipt images you upload. Usage data: basic sign-in and device information needed to keep your account secure.

2. How We Use It

To provide the App's core features — dashboards, reports, budgets, reminders and notifications. To auto-generate salary and recurring-expense entries you've configured. To keep your account secure and communicate important account or security notices.

3. Where It's Stored

Your data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database with row-level security, meaning only you can read or write your own records — not other users, and not app administrators through normal use of the App. Uploaded receipts are stored in a private storage bucket, accessible only via short-lived signed links generated for you.

4. Who We Share It With

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the infrastructure providers needed to run the App (currently Supabase for database/auth/storage and Vercel for hosting), and only to the extent needed to provide the service.

5. Your Rights

You can export your data at any time from Export Data (CSV). You can correct your profile information at any time from Profile & Security. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile & Security → Danger Zone — this cannot be undone.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is permanently removed, including uploaded receipts.

7. Security

We use industry-standard practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS), row-level database security, and private storage for receipts. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age required by their local law to consent to data processing.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
